# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript portfolio site served directly in the browser.

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and clients evaluating the candidate's profile, technical background, and communication skills.

## Product Purpose

Present Breno De Almeida Santos as a technology student with a strong foundation in systems analysis, development, networking, and professional growth. The portfolio helps visitors understand his background, skills, and project experience quickly.

## Positioning

This is a personal portfolio that communicates technical competence, practical learning, and a foundation in software development and network security. It differentiates itself through a clear professional narrative and a modern portfolio presentation aimed at digital opportunities.

## Operating Context

Used in browser-based contexts such as personal portfolio review, professional networking, recruitment screening, and project inquiry. The content is read on desktop and mobile, with emphasis on accessibility and clear navigation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page portfolio with sections for overview, skills, projects, education, and contact.
- Static front-end implementation with Bootstrap and custom CSS.
- Contact form is front-end validation only and not connected to a backend service.
- Content is written in Portuguese and presented for a professional personal brand.

## Brand Commitments

The site should project a modern, technical, and trustworthy identity aligned with software development, systems analysis, and network security.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing site content in the repository.
- Academic and professional information already present in the portfolio sections.
- Project and course descriptions recorded in the current HTML copy.

## Product Principles

- Clarity before decoration.
- Technical credibility over visual noise.
- Modern usability across desktop and mobile screens.
- A professional and approachable personal brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Use readable contrast and keyboard-friendly navigation.
- Maintain semantic section structure and labels for forms and links.
- Ensure responsive behavior for mobile users and support readable typography sizes.
